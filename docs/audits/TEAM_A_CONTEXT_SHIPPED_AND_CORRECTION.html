"Barry AI Insights" — the surrounding UI

A card in the Cadences dashboard, beside a "View all performance" panel. Header row: the label, then a purple BETA pill. Body switches on whether there is data:

  • Empty state — Barry's avatar at 48px, 70% opacity, above: "As your cadences collect data, Barry will surface insights and recommend next actions."
  • Populated — a stack of InsightRows, each an icon, a sentence and an action link. Example: "3 contact(s) replied to your cadences. Consider moving them to a Campaign to continue the conversation." → "View replied contacts", which filters the table below.

What it actually does: counts replies, opens-without-reply and failures across the user's cadences, then offers a filter action for each. It is a computed summary with next actions — not a generated narrative.

Recommendation: Barry's Read. "Insights" oversells three counted states, and the card's own empty copy already promises insights later ("will surface insights"), which sits oddly under a heading claiming them now. "Barry's Read" matches what is there — a short interpretation plus a suggested next move — and stays true when the BETA pill comes off. Barry's Insights is the safer choice if you would rather not introduce a new noun.
