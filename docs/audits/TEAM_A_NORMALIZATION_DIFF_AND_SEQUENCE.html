What the sequence does wrong, read as one experience

The two surfaces tell different stories. Surface A is Barry in first person, doing work for you, and it lands well until the word "engine". Surface B narrates Barry in the third person as a system you configure and train — "train Barry AI", "Barry executes it", "the smarter Barry gets". A user who sees both meets two different Barrys: a colleague and a machine.

Every flag above falls into one of three patterns:

  • Autonomous system — "prospecting engine", "Barry executes it in Scout"
  • Training / surveillance — "train Barry AI", "Recon teaches Barry", "the smarter Barry gets"
  • Generic AI-does-X — "AI analyzes your inputs and finds patterns", "Barry AI"

Step 2's progress list is a borderline case I would leave alone: "Analyzing website" and "Crafting your value props" describe literal work in progress and read as competence, not autonomy. Worth a decision rather than an assumption.

New structural finding — training framing is not a string problem

"Train Barry" is not two lines in an explainer. It is the organizing metaphor of the entire RECON module, and it is built into structure you have frozen:

(Section1Foundation.jsx:347) · RECON's own greeting: "Complete the training modules below" (ReconOnboardingWizard.jsx:55).

The visible labels can all move without touching the route or the filename. But this is a module-level renaming decision — what RECON is, if it is not training — and it is bigger than Batch 0b. Recommend scoping it separately rather than folding it in. If Barry connects context, RECON is plausibly where you give him context; but that is your call to make, not a string swap.
