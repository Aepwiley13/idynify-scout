# #003.6 — Unified Navigation (read-only audit and proposal)

**Status: design direction approved (003.6a + 003.6b). Implementation is gated on Contact Hub integration.** Coordination: `003.6-contact-hub-coordination.md`. Audited on `main` at `dcc19e54`.
Visual concepts at laptop, desktop and compact widths: the "Unified Navigation" artifact.

## Today: two navigation layers on desktop

| Layer | Component | Width | What it lists |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Sidebar` (wide / compact) | 220px / 64px | Modules: Mission Control, Pipeline (Scout, Hunter, Sniper), Relationships (Basecamp, Reinforcements, Fallback), Intelligence (Recon), Command Center; Barry in a pinned footer |
| 2 | `ModuleSubNav` panel, per module | 190px (collapsible) | The active module's sections: Hunter 13 (with Blitz), Recon 9, Command Center 9, Scout 7, Sniper 7, Reinforcements 5, Basecamp 4, Fallback 3; also Settings 7 |

On module pages the two layers take 410px: **32% of a 1280 screen**. Workspace is 870px at 1280,
1030px at 1440, 1510px at 1920.

## The standard: Contact Hub's expandable sidebar

On the Contact Hub candidate (`integration/contact-hub-unified-qa` @ `2f4697fa`, not on `main`):
- `components/layout/sidebarSlots.js`: a small store. While a module is active **and the sidebar is
  wide**, `Sidebar` draws one empty `.sidebar-subnav` element under that module's entry and
  registers it. The module portals its own entries into it (`HubSidebarNav`). The sidebar loads
  nothing for the module, and an inactive module costs nothing.
- Compact sidebar, phone, or a page outside the shell: there's no slot, so the page's own navigation applies.
- Styling: indented 20px behind a 1px rule; 36px rows; 13px secondary text; counts at 11px tabular;
  purple tint and weight 650 on the active item; long lists scroll on their own (`max-height:
  min(38vh, 300px)`).

## Proposal

**003.6a: wide sidebar, sections nested for every module.**
- Open the slot to every module, not just Contact Hub. `ModuleSubNav` draws its existing items into
  the slot when one is offered, and keeps its panel when not. Every module's `*_ITEMS`, routes,
  handlers, counts (Command Center's active-mission badge), order and active state are reused as they are.
- While nested, the 190px panel isn't rendered. **+190px** on every module page: 1060 / 1220 / 1700px
  at 1280 / 1440 / 1920 (+22% / +18% / +13%).
- Only the active module expands. Hunter is grouped into **Daily Engagement** (All People, Follow Up Now, Today's Actions, Replied, Active, New, Companies) and **Outreach Management** (Campaigns, Cadences, Missions, Mission Outcomes), with Blitz Mode first.
  The sidebar's module list already scrolls, and Barry stays in the pinned footer.
- Styling reuses Contact Hub's nested-entry rules, shared rather than copied.

**003.6b: compact rail, section flyout** (recommended over a fallback panel). With the rail at
64px, a click or keyboard activation on the active module opens a flyout listing its sections.
Esc and clicking outside close it, and focus returns to the rail item. Workspace becomes 1216px at
1280. The fallback panel (Option A, 1026px) would make compact mode narrower than wide mode.

**Unchanged:** all routes, destinations and permission checks; mobile (drawer for modules, bottom
bar for sections); Settings keeps its in-page section list; Mission Control and pages without a
panel.

**Absorbs:** #003 (compact labels) and most of #004.4 (laptop chrome).

## Gate and risks

1. **Contact Hub merges first.** The slot mechanism and Contact Hub's `Sidebar` changes exist only on
   its candidate branch. A parallel implementation on `main` would conflict. The order is: Contact Hub
   merges, then 003.6 generalizes its slot. Coordinate with that team.
2. **That candidate is older than #001.** Its `Sidebar.jsx` / `Sidebar.css` diff restores the
   pre-#001 wordmark sizing (204×68 box). #001's brand sizing must be kept when it merges.
3. **Tests pin section panels** (`moduleMigration`, `scoutSubNav`, the `hunter*` suites).
   They'll need to assert sections in the sidebar slot in wide mode, and in the panel otherwise.
4. **Sidebar height.** Hunter expanded adds about 13 rows. The grouping, the existing nav scroll and
   the pinned footer keep every module and Barry reachable; verify at 1280×720.

| Phase | Impact | Effort | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 003.6a wide sidebar nesting | High (+190px on every module page) | Medium | Medium |
| 003.6b compact flyout | Medium (+190px in compact mode) | Medium | Medium (keyboard and focus behaviour) |

## Approved requirements

- Generalize Contact Hub's existing slot mechanism; don't duplicate it.
- Keep #001 branding and #002 typography.
- Hunter groups: Daily Engagement and Outreach Management.
- Keep every destination, badge, permission and behaviour.
- Barry stays pinned and reachable.
- Compact flyouts are keyboard-accessible: open with Enter/Space, arrow keys move between items,
  Esc closes, and focus returns to the rail item.
- Navigation scrolls independently of the workspace.
- Mobile navigation is unchanged.
- Every module is verified at desktop (1920) and laptop (1280×720) sizes.
